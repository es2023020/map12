--- PAGE 5 ---
5
Despite its vast scale, only 15% of the area is developed, 
ensuring a harmonious blend with the natural surroundings, 
and each of its nine rows is meticulously designed 
and constructed across multiple levels. One of Safia’s 
highlights is its impressive 750-meter beachfront, offering 
unparalleled views and elevations ranging from 3m to 40m 
above sea level to ensure a full sea view and access to 
the sea. With a project depth of 1500 meters, the project 
incorporates water features such as tranquil lagoons 
and swimming pools. The design creates a diversified 
environment that combines spacious landscapes with 
water features, promising residents an unparalleled living 
experience by the sea.
Safia’s project spans over 
180 acres.

UNIQUE 
SANDY BEACH
At Safia, residents can enjoy its exclusive sandy beach, stretched along the 
expansive Mediterranean coastline. This heavenly white shoreline offers 
residents a serene escape unlike any other. Whether you’re strolling along the 
soft sands, enjoying a refreshing swim in the crystal-clear waters, or simply 
relaxing under the sun, our private beach provides an unmatched, lavish 
experience.
Safia reflects peace of living, the experience of seamlessly 
integrating water features and lush landscapes, creating an 
environment that offers breathtaking views. These beautiful 
settings include commercial and shopping areas, providing 
a harmonious blend of nature and urban convenience for an 
exceptional lifestyle experience. Eateries are surrounded 
by landscape, offering delightful spots to enjoy a meal or a 
cup of coffee while immersing in the serene surroundings 
and sea breeze. Every detail in Safia is meticulously planned 
to ensure that residents can lead a balanced and fulfilling, 
enjoyable living experience.

DINE AND SHINE
Experienced with top-notch hospitality, Safia stands out not only as a premier 
Mediterranean coastal destination but also as a pioneer in hospitality, with one of 
the first hotels in Ras El Hekma. Located 31 meters above sea level, this distinctive 
hotel provides unparalleled panoramic sea views, offering guests a serene and 
elevated perspective of the clear white beaches and crystal blue sea.
Explore Safia’s culinary experience that blends international flavors with local 
authenticity, available around the clock for our residents convenience. Additionally, 
residents can explore a curated selection of high-end local brands offering 
international appeal, perfect for those seeking unique shopping experiences.
