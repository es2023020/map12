03.The Architecture

--- PAGE 11 ---
Inspired By The Mediterranean, C North's Architecture Is Shaped By Its 
Surroundings. Lush Landscapes Soften The Built Environment, Expansive 
Openings Invite Natural Light, And Every Elevation Is Carefully Oriented 
Toward The Sea.
A Palette Of Warm, Timeless Materials, Drawn From Sand, Stone, And The 
Shoreline, Creates Homes That Feel Effortlessly Connected To Nature.
A Design Language in Harmony
With The Shore

05.Masterplan

--- PAGE 18 ---
Spanning 114 Feddans Along The Pristine Coastline At 188 Km, C North Is Masterfully 
Designed To Celebrate Space, Serenity, And Sea. Built On Levels, The Elevation 
Across The Project Guarantees Uninterrupted Sea Views From Every Corner.
With Only 15% Of The Land Developed, The Remaining Space Is Devoted To Lush 
Landscapes, Open-Air Experiences, And Natural Flow—Allowing Every Element To 
Breathe. The Masterplan Is A Deliberate Balance Between Refined Architecture And 
The Raw Beauty Of Ras El Hekma’s Shores.
